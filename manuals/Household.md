# Cleaning

## Limescale
Acids are best for cleaning the limescale. Just use vinegar on the affected area, prolong exposure is better.


## Stains
Best is the [Gall Soap](https://cs.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C5%BDlu%C4%8Dov%C3%A9_m%C3%BDdlo). Especially good for oil stains, proteins, or nature pigments.
